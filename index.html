About Me

Started to learn web development just to take up an in-demand occupation I have found myself (to my surprise!) passionate about this magic world of code and algorithms. Yes, I'm not mistaken, the coding is really magic. Otherwise, how to explain that it captured me with the first "Hello World" I displayed on the screen?

One of great merits of web development for me - you will never be bored with it. It's like a big puzzle you're grappling with, that makes you think about it day and night. And what's the most important - through solving these diverse problems web development fosters long-term learning and honing my skills.

If I'm not coding, you can find me discovering new amazing spots near my home or far away. Or baking delicious compound deserts in my kitchen. Or skiing high up in mountains. Or just reading a book lying in the bed. Oh, I almost forgot, I adore driving my car, and, in my immodest opinion, I'm good enough at it.
